The portfolio

The firm’s capital at work — one development in progress and completed transactions. Every entry was underwritten by A.E.F., funded with the firm’s own capital, and executed in partnership with established local operators, from entry to exit.

Ground-up development · Residential · In progress

Le Ville della Marcigliana — Bufalotta, Rome

Ten single-family villas of c. 250 m² gross over three levels, on the last developable land adjoining the Marcigliana Nature Reserve on the northern edge of Rome. Every villa backs onto parkland protected by statute — a boundary fixed by law, not by planning cycles. Design is complete and consent is progressing; works are programmed for 2027, with completion expected by year-end.

Value-add · Commercial · Completed

Viale Ciamarra — Rome

A 400 m² commercial unit acquired privately in a distressed situation in June 2025, in partnership with a local operator. The unit was re-let to a stronger tenant, refinanced at month six — releasing the firm’s capital while the asset was still held — and sold in May 2026 at €950,000: a 42% return on capital in eleven months.

Villa Morena — Rome

A 210 m² detached villa secured off-market in a distressed situation in December 2024, together with the firm’s local operating partner. Full internal renovation and new landscaping were delivered in three months, and the asset was sold on completion in April 2025 — a 34% return on capital in under five months, door to door.
