About the firm

who we are

Investor, developer, landlord, manager.

A.E.F. Realty Ltd. is a private real estate investment vehicle. The firm develops commercial and residential buildings, lets and operates its own and leased real estate, and manages every asset it owns directly, in-house.

We are deliberately small, deliberately private and deliberately patient. We take on few projects, and we stay with them.

Guiding principles

01

Underwrite like owners

Every decision — price, design, tenants — is taken with our own capital at stake, on buildings we must be ready to hold longer than planned. Discipline first.

02

Quality as economics

Doing it properly once costs less than doing it twice. Specification decisions are investment decisions.

03

Alignment of interests

We put our own capital where our convictions are. When we manage for others, we treat their assets like ours.

04

Discretion and integrity

Real estate is a business of long relationships. We keep our word, our deadlines and our counsel.

05

Care for what exists

The most sustainable building is the one that is kept in use. We maintain, adapt and renew before we replace.

background
