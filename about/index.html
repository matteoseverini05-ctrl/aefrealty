About the firm

who we are

Principal capital, local execution.

A.E.F. Realty Ltd is an independent real estate investment firm. We underwrite and fund value-add, development and credit transactions, executed on the ground with established operating partners — one venture per asset, from acquisition to exit.

We are a young firm — deliberately small, deliberately private and deliberately patient. We take on few transactions, commit our own capital to each, and stay with them.

Guiding principles

01

Underwrite like owners

Every decision — price, design, tenants — is taken with our own capital at stake, on buildings we must be ready to hold longer than planned. Discipline first.

02

Quality as economics

Doing it properly once costs less than doing it twice. Specification decisions are investment decisions.

03

Alignment of interests

Our capital is in every venture we underwrite, and our operating partners are invested alongside. Nobody in the room is unaligned.

04

Discretion and integrity

Real estate is a business of long relationships. We keep our word, our deadlines and our counsel.

05

Care for what exists

The most sustainable building is the one that is kept in use. We maintain, adapt and renew before we replace.

background

The firm today.

A.E.F. Realty is a young firm — and deliberately so. It carries no legacy structures, no inherited portfolio and no habits formed in easier markets. What it does carry is an old discipline: acquisition at the right basis, value created through work, exits taken when the plan says so.

From inception, €7.8M of real estate has passed through that discipline in three years, with returns measured in months rather than cycles.
