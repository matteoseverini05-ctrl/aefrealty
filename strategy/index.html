Investment strategy

how we invest

Three strategies across the risk spectrum.

The firm operates across three strategies in a single asset class, deploying capital where intervention creates value: buildings to requalify and re-let, sites to develop, credits whose recovery runs through the underlying property.

Each strategy is underwritten by the firm and executed with established local operating partners — one venture, one plan, from acquisition to disposal.

strategy 01

Value-add

We buy buildings with a problem to solve — vacant, tired, under-managed — in prime and non-prime locations alike. We requalify them, bring them to income with new tenants, and sell once the value has been created and stabilised.

Acquisition. Prime and non-prime locations. What matters is not the postcode alone but the value gap we can close.
Requalification. Design, permits and works run with the operating partner’s construction team — on budget, on programme.
Lease-up & exit. New tenants, stabilised income, disciplined sale. The plan is written before we buy.

strategy 02

Development

We develop from the ground up, across the commercial and residential sectors. Land or obsolete assets in; finished, income-ready buildings out. Development is where our construction discipline earns its keep — and where the widest value spreads live.

Feasibility first. Planning strategy and hard numbers before a single drawing is commissioned.
Partnered delivery. Construction managed day by day by the local operating partner, with the firm’s cost and programme control at every stage.
Exit options. Sell on completion, or let and hold while the market matures — each scheme carries both routes.

strategy 03

Real estate credit

We acquire distressed credits with real estate underlying. Where others see a non-performing loan, we underwrite the building behind it — because we have the skills to take that building, requalify it, let it and sell it if that is what recovery takes. Credit is another door into the same asset.

Secured positions. Credits backed by tangible property, valued on the real estate — not the paper.
Workout capability. Through regulated servicers and its operating partners, the firm can run the full journey from credit to asset to exit.
Price discipline. Bought at the right discount, the downside is the building itself. We know buildings.

execution

Four operating capabilities.

Every strategy above is executed through four operating capabilities, delivered with the firm’s local partners: commercial and residential construction, letting & operation, and property management.
