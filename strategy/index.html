Investment strategy

how we invest

Three strategies, one asset class.

We deploy capital where intervention creates value: buildings to requalify and re-let, sites to develop, credits whose recovery runs through the property behind them.

We do not run a fund with a mandate to fill. We take the transaction the market offers — if the price is right and the plan is written before we buy.

Villa Morena, Rome — value-add, completed 2025
strategy 01

Value-add

Mispriced or under-managed assets — vacant, tired, capital-starved — bought at the right basis, repositioned, returned to income and sold once value is stabilised.

Acquisition. Prime and non-prime alike. What matters is the value gap we can close, not the postcode alone.
Requalification. Design, permits and works, delivered by the operating partner on budget and on programme.
Lease-up & exit. New tenants, stabilised income, disciplined sale.

Le Ville della Marcigliana, Rome — development, indicative CGI
strategy 02

Development

Ground-up development, commercial and residential. Land or obsolete assets in; finished, income-ready buildings out.

Feasibility first. Planning strategy and hard numbers before a single drawing is commissioned.
Partnered delivery. Construction run day by day by the local operating partner, with the firm’s cost and programme control at every stage.
Two exits. Sell on completion, or let and hold while the market matures — every scheme is underwritten for both.

strategy 03

Real estate credit

Distressed credits with real estate underlying, acquired through regulated servicers. Where others see a non-performing loan, we underwrite the building behind it — and, with our partners, can take it, requalify it, let it and sell it if that is what recovery takes.

Secured positions. Credits backed by tangible property, valued on the real estate — not the paper.
Workout capability. Regulated servicers and operating partners carry the position from credit to asset to exit.
Price discipline. Bought at the right discount, the downside is the building itself. We know buildings.

execution

Underwritten here, delivered there.

Every venture is delivered on the ground by an established local operating partner — construction, letting, management — with the firm’s cost, programme and exit control at every stage. Own capital in, one plan, one accountable partnership from acquisition to disposal.
