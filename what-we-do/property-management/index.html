Property management

capability 04

Property management

We manage what we own. Every asset in the portfolio is run directly by the firm — maintenance, tenants, costs, works — with no outsourced responsibility. Keeping ownership and management under the same roof is what keeps buildings, and returns, in shape.

our approach
01

Owner-managed

Every building the firm holds is managed directly. Accountability stays in-house, from acquisition to disposal.

02

Operating discipline

Occupancy, arrears, costs and planned works are tracked continuously, with the same rigour as any investment decision.

03

Value preservation

Preventive maintenance and planned works, so buildings appreciate instead of decay.
