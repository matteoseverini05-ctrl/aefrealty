Privacy notice

how we handle personal data
Last updated — 5 September 2026

This notice explains how A.E.F. Realty Ltd (“A.E.F. Realty”, “we”) handles personal data in connection with this website and with correspondence sent to the firm. A.E.F. Realty Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under Company No. 14876988, with its registered office at 114 Sinclair Road, London W14 0NL, United Kingdom. For anything in this notice, write to info@aefrealty.co.uk. We are the controller of the personal data described below.

What we collect

Two things, and little else. First, correspondence: if you write to us, we hold your name, your email address and the content of your message. Second, technical data: the provider that hosts this website keeps standard server logs — IP address, pages requested, browser type and time of request — for security and to keep the site running.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies. To understand how the site is used, we rely on Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first measurement service that works without cookies, without local storage and without fingerprinting: it gives us aggregate figures only — pages viewed, referrers, countries and device types — and is not capable of identifying or following an individual visitor. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking technology of any kind.

Why we use your data

We use correspondence to respond to you and to take whatever steps you ask of us before entering into a relationship or a transaction. The legal bases are our legitimate interest in conducting the firm’s business and, where relevant, steps taken at your request prior to a contract. We do not use your data for marketing, we do not profile you, and no decision about you is made by automated means.

Who sees it

Our IT service providers — email, website hosting and traffic measurement (Cloudflare) — process data on our behalf under contract. Professional advisers see correspondence where a matter requires it, and authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it is processed

Our providers process data in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. If any processing takes place outside those territories, we rely on recognised safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as the enquiry or relationship requires — as a rule, no longer than twenty-four months after our last exchange, unless a transaction follows or the law requires a longer period. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider for short, standard periods.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR — and, where it applies to you, the EU GDPR — you may ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, a restriction on its use, a copy in portable form, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to info@aefrealty.co.uk and we will respond within a month. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or, in the EU, to your local supervisory authority.

Changes

If this notice changes, the current version will always be at this address, with the date above updated.
